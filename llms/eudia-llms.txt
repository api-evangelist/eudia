# Eudia

> Eudia is an enterprise augmented-intelligence platform for in-house legal, compliance, and procurement teams. Its "Enterprise Brain" ingests an organization's contracts, policies, precedents, and prior decisions and codifies the judgment of its legal experts into governed AI workflows and "Expert Digital Twins." Founded in 2023 and based in Palo Alto, California; backed by General Catalyst and Sierra Ventures. Eudia does not publish a public developer API or documentation; integrations are delivered through connectors to enterprise systems.
